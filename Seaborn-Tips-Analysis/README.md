# Seaborn Tips Analysis

## Project Overview
An Exploratory Data Analysis and Data Visualization project using the Seaborn Tips dataset to understand restaurant billing patterns and customer tipping behaviour.

## Objectives
- Identify which day has the highest average restaurant bill.
- Compare average tips between smokers and non-smokers.
- Explore the relationship between total bill and tip amount.
- Compare the number of recorded transactions during lunch and dinner.

## Tools & Libraries
- Python
- Pandas
- Seaborn
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

## Visualizations
- Bar Plot: Average restaurant bill by day.
- Bar Plot: Comparison of average tips by smoking status.
- Scatter Plot: Relationship between total bill and tip amount.
- Heatmap: Percentage distribution of lunch and dinner transactions.

## Key Findings
1. Sunday recorded the highest average restaurant bill.
2. Smokers and non-smokers showed differences in their average tipping amounts.
3. Total bill and tip amount showed a positive relationship.
4. Dinner accounted for 72.1% of recorded transactions, while lunch accounted for 27.9%.

## Conclusion
The visualizations helped identify patterns in restaurant bills, tipping behaviour and meal-time transactions. This project provided practical experience in data exploration and visualization using Python and Seaborn.
